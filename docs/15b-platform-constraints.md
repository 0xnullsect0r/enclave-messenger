# Platform Constraint Register

Status: Draft (M0) · Normative

Source: PLAN.md §10, §13, §14, §17, §18, §22, Appendix A. This register lists every platform rule or limit that shapes Enclave's design. Each row has a status from PLAN Appendix A (**V** verified, **V\*** standard value from memory, **U** unresolved with an assigned check) and the milestone where it is checked or re-checked. Design responses MUST NOT assume a constraint away; if a constraint changes, update this register first.

## Open questions

1. **Resolved:** both fallbacks are built, so no spike result is needed. PC-03: when CallKit refuses an incoming call, including where it isn't allowed, the app posts a regular "Incoming call" notification (`enclave-platform` `phone/ios/calls.rs` `incoming_now`). PC-20: the service is typed `specialUse` with its stated purpose (`AndroidManifest.xml`, `EnclaveService.java`), and the Play build also offers Google's push with dummy wakes (`Fcm.java`, `10-push.md`). Whether Apple accepts the China storefront and Play accepts `specialUse` is decided at review (G3, G4).
2. **Resolved:** neither per-manufacturer screens nor a link out. Per-OEM screens go stale, and opening an outside page tells that site who uses Enclave. The app will show one generic in-app explanation and open Android's own battery-optimization setting for Enclave. Built: Settings explains it when Android may stop Enclave, and opens that setting (`openBatterySettings`).
3. **Resolved:** refused by the store package, warned in the others. The MSIX needs Windows 10 version 2004 (`MinVersion` 10.0.19041.0); the `.msi` and setup `.exe` still install on older versions, and there Settings says screen capture can't be hidden, because `SetWindowDisplayAffinity` with `WDA_EXCLUDEFROMCAPTURE` fails (`apply_screen_security`).
